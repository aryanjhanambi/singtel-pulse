05 / WHAT WE CAN SEE

Scope of coverage

✓
Public web endpoints

We request Singtel’s homepage and support page, plus Fanfiction.net. Three failed checks flag a site; two successful checks clear it.

✓
RIPE network signals

Atlas reports volunteer probes connected through AS9506. RIS shows prefixes visible to its routing collectors; its data updates about every eight hours. A majority of at least four recently active Atlas probes must disconnect before we flag that signal.

—
Limits of these checks

A 403 may be bot protection. Probe disconnections and BGP visibility do not directly measure every Singtel customer's connection or mobile service. Downdetector is a manual cross-check.

i

This dashboard reports observed signals, not a confirmed nationwide outage.
